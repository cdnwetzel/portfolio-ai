# Memorial Day Is a Pause to Remember the People

*LinkedIn post by Chris Wetzel, published 2026-05-25. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chris-wetzel_memorial-day-isnt-a-summary-of-sacrifice-activity-7464646587626184704-09Rp*

Memorial Day isn't a summary of sacrifice. It's a pause to remember the people.

Not the idea of service. The actual person who left home knowing they might not come back. The actual choice they made, knowing the cost.

Most days we talk about what we should do, what we claim to value, what we intend to change. Today isn't one of those days.

Today is for remembering the ones who didn't get to come home and decide what came next. Whose last choice was already made. Whose absence is still the price.

Honor them by holding their memory clearly. Not as inspiration. Not as motivation for your goals. Just as what it was: someone's life, someone's sacrifice, the actual weight of it.
